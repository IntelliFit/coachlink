# batch-10 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, 3-key-frame horizontal image, action-only red/orange highlights.

## 046 高脚杯深蹲
- Camera/view: front 3/4, full body.
- Equipment: kettlebell or dumbbell held vertically at chest.
- Frame 1: standing, feet shoulder-width, weight at sternum.
- Frame 2: descent, hips back, knees bend, torso upright.
- Frame 3: standing finish, hips/knees extended.
- Primary red: 股四头肌.
- Secondary orange: 臀大肌, 核心.
- Avoid: spine rounding, knee collapse.

## 047 高位下拉
- Camera/view: front seated upper-body dominant.
- Equipment: lat pulldown machine, wide bar, thigh pad.
- Frame 1: arms extended overhead, torso upright.
- Frame 2: elbows drive down/back, bar descends.
- Frame 3: bar near upper chest, scapulae depressed/retracted.
- Primary red: 背阔肌.
- Secondary orange: 肱二头.
- Avoid: behind-neck pull, machine labels.

## 048 跪姿俯卧撑
- Camera/view: side 3/4, full body knees to head.
- Equipment: mat only.
- Frame 1: kneeling plank top, hands under shoulders.
- Frame 2: chest lowers toward floor.
- Frame 3: press back to top.
- Primary red: 胸大肌.
- Secondary orange: 三头肌, 前三角.
- Avoid: hip sag, hands too far forward.

## 049 击掌俯卧撑
- Camera/view: side 3/4 dynamic, full body.
- Equipment: floor/mat.
- Frame 1: push-up bottom-to-mid start.
- Frame 2: explosive airborne clap.
- Frame 3: landing with elbows soft and body aligned.
- Primary red: 胸大肌.
- Secondary orange: 三头肌, 肩部.
- Avoid: cartoon motion streaks, text effects.

## 050 架上拉
- Camera/view: side 3/4, rack pins visible.
- Equipment: barbell on power-rack pins around knee height.
- Frame 1: grip bar on pins, hips hinged, spine neutral.
- Frame 2: bar leaves pins, torso extends, hips drive forward.
- Frame 3: lockout, standing tall, bar at thighs.
- Primary red: 竖脊肌.
- Secondary orange: 斜方肌, 臀大肌.
- Avoid: rounded back, rack labels, bulky straps.
