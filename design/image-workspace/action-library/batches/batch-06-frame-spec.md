# batch-06 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, 3-key-frame triptych, no text/labels/watermark.

## 026 传统硬拉
- Camera/view: front 3/4 low camera, full body.
- Equipment: Olympic barbell with plates on floor.
- Frame 1: setup, feet hip-width, hips hinged, knees slightly bent, hands outside legs, neutral spine.
- Frame 2: bar passing knees, hips and shoulders rise together.
- Frame 3: lockout, standing tall, hips extended, bar at thighs.
- Primary red: 臀大肌, 腘绳肌.
- Secondary orange: 背部.
- Avoid: rounded spine, squat-like start.

## 027 锤式弯举
- Camera/view: front slight 3/4, torso/full body.
- Equipment: dumbbells, neutral grip.
- Frame 1: arms extended at sides, palms inward.
- Frame 2: elbows pinned, forearms halfway up.
- Frame 3: dumbbells near shoulders, upper arms vertical.
- Primary red: 肱桡肌, 肱肌.
- Secondary orange: 肱二头肌.
- Avoid: torso swing, shoulder raise.

## 028 单臂哑铃划船
- Camera/view: side 3/4 bench perspective.
- Equipment: flat bench, one dumbbell.
- Frame 1: knee/hand braced on bench, working arm extended down.
- Frame 2: elbow drives back, dumbbell near lower ribs.
- Frame 3: elbow behind torso, dumbbell close to hip/rib line.
- Primary red: 背阔肌.
- Secondary orange: 菱形肌, 肱二头肌.
- Avoid: torso twist, rounded back.

## 029 单腿深蹲
- Camera/view: front 3/4, full body.
- Equipment: bodyweight.
- Frame 1: stand on one leg, free leg forward, arms forward.
- Frame 2: stance knee flexes, hips back/down.
- Frame 3: deep single-leg squat, stance foot flat.
- Primary red: 股四头肌, 臀大肌.
- Secondary orange: 核心.
- Avoid: knee collapse, hand support.

## 030 弹力带坐姿划船
- Camera/view: front 3/4 seated.
- Equipment: band anchored in front or looped around feet.
- Frame 1: legs extended, arms straight holding band.
- Frame 2: elbows move back, scapulae retracting.
- Frame 3: handles near lower ribs, elbows behind torso.
- Primary red: 背阔肌.
- Secondary orange: 中下斜方肌.
- Avoid: cable machine, excessive lean-back.
