# batch-05 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, 3-key-frame strip, red primary/orange secondary action highlights only.

## 021 吊环团身引体
- Camera/view: front 3/4, rings overhead, full body.
- Equipment: gymnastic rings with black straps.
- Frame 1: dead hang from rings, arms extended, knees tucked.
- Frame 2: pull midpoint, elbows bending, torso rising, tuck held.
- Frame 3: top finish, chin near ring line, elbows close to ribs.
- Primary red: lats/upper back, arm flexors, core.
- Secondary orange: forearms, hip flexors, shoulder stabilizers.
- Avoid: straight-leg pull-up, shoes, chest-dominant marking.

## 022 吊环引体
- Camera/view: front 3/4, vertical full-body.
- Equipment: rings.
- Frame 1: dead hang, arms extended, legs straight.
- Frame 2: elbows around 90 degrees, rings pulled close, shoulder blades down/back.
- Frame 3: chest lifted toward rings, chin above hand level.
- Primary red: lats/upper back, biceps/brachialis.
- Secondary orange: forearms, lower traps/rhomboids, core, chest stabilizers.
- Avoid: kipping, tucked knees, swinging.

## 023 阿诺德推举
- Camera/view: standing or seated front 3/4, torso and arms clear.
- Equipment: pair of dumbbells.
- Frame 1: dumbbells before shoulders, palms facing body, elbows forward.
- Frame 2: elbows rotate outward, dumbbells beside head, palms turning forward.
- Frame 3: overhead lockout, dumbbells above shoulders.
- Primary red: anterior/lateral deltoids, triceps.
- Secondary orange: upper chest, traps/serratus, core.
- Avoid: barbell, excessive lean back, bench blocking anatomy.

## 024 保加利亚分腿蹲
- Camera/view: side 3/4, lower body clear.
- Equipment: flat bench behind body.
- Frame 1: rear foot elevated, front foot planted, torso upright.
- Frame 2: front knee bends, rear knee drops, hips lower.
- Frame 3: front leg drives upward, hip/knee extend.
- Primary red: front-leg quadriceps, gluteus maximus.
- Secondary orange: hamstrings, adductors, calves, core.
- Avoid: heel lift, knee collapse, dumbbells hiding legs.

## 025 爆发式俯卧撑
- Camera/view: side 3/4 low camera.
- Equipment: floor only.
- Frame 1: plank push-up top/start.
- Frame 2: chest near floor, elbows bent, core braced.
- Frame 3: explosive push, hands briefly off floor.
- Primary red: pectoralis major, triceps, core.
- Secondary orange: anterior deltoids, serratus, forearms.
- Avoid: clap obscuring anatomy, sagging hips, motion blur.
