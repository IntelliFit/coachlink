# batch-04 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, rings black/dark gray, 3-key-frame horizontal image, no text/labels/watermark.

## 016 吊环单手俯卧撑
- Camera/view: front 3/4 low view, body diagonal.
- Equipment: low rings, vertical straps.
- Frame 1: one hand on ring, free arm behind back, feet wide plank.
- Frame 2: chest lowers toward ring, working elbow bent, torso rigid.
- Frame 3: arm presses nearly straight, returns to plank.
- Primary red: chest.
- Secondary orange: anterior shoulder, triceps.
- Avoid: two-hand appearance, torso twist.

## 017 吊环俯卧撑可加负重
- Camera/view: side/front 3/4, full body horizontal.
- Equipment: low rings, compact plate/vest on upper back.
- Frame 1: plank on rings, elbows extended.
- Frame 2: chest descends between rings, elbows bend.
- Frame 3: press to straight-arm plank.
- Primary red: chest.
- Secondary orange: anterior shoulders, triceps.
- Avoid: bench push-up, knees down, text on weight.

## 018 吊环靠墙倒立俯卧撑
- Camera/view: side 3/4, wall behind feet.
- Equipment: low rings/blocks, wall support.
- Frame 1: wall-supported handstand, hands on rings, elbows extended.
- Frame 2: head/upper body lowers between rings, elbows bent, core braced.
- Frame 3: press to tall handstand.
- Primary red: shoulders.
- Secondary orange: triceps, core.
- Avoid: freestanding/no wall, pike push-up, chest dominance.

## 019 吊环屈臂撑
- Camera/view: front 3/4, torso upright/slightly forward.
- Equipment: rings at dip height.
- Frame 1: support above rings, arms straight, shoulders depressed.
- Frame 2: elbows bend, torso leans forward, chest descends.
- Frame 3: press to top support.
- Primary red: chest.
- Secondary orange: subtle triceps/anterior delts if needed.
- Avoid: parallel bars, pull-up posture, feet touching floor.

## 020 吊环团身俯卧撑
- Camera/view: front/side 3/4 showing tuck.
- Equipment: low rings.
- Frame 1: tucked plank, hands on rings, knees toward chest.
- Frame 2: elbows bend while knees stay tucked, chest lowers.
- Frame 3: press up maintaining tuck.
- Primary red: chest.
- Secondary orange: anterior shoulders, triceps, core.
- Avoid: straight-leg push-up, knees on ground, extra colors.
