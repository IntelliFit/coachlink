# batch-07 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, one horizontal 3-key-frame sheet per action, no text/labels/watermark.

## 031 地板卧推
- Camera/view: front 3/4 from foot-end, low floor perspective.
- Equipment: floor mat, barbell or dumbbells.
- Frame 1: supine, knees bent, elbows on floor, forearms vertical, weight above chest line.
- Frame 2: press midpoint, elbows leaving floor, ribs neutral.
- Frame 3: arms extended over chest, wrists stacked.
- Primary red: 胸大肌.
- Secondary orange: 肱三头肌.
- Avoid: bench, shoes, extra highlights.

## 032 登山跑慢速版
- Camera/view: side 3/4 at low torso height.
- Equipment: floor mat.
- Frame 1: high plank, shoulders over wrists.
- Frame 2: one knee drives slowly toward chest.
- Frame 3: switch side, opposite knee under torso.
- Primary red: 核心.
- Secondary orange: 肩部, 下肢.
- Avoid: running blur, speed lines, spine collapse.

## 033 低杠深蹲
- Camera/view: rear 3/4 showing hip hinge and bar path.
- Equipment: low-bar barbell.
- Frame 1: standing setup, bar low on upper back.
- Frame 2: bottom/eccentric, hips back, torso inclined, spine neutral.
- Frame 3: standing finish, bar over midfoot.
- Primary red: 股四头肌, 臀大肌.
- Secondary orange: 竖脊肌.
- Avoid: high-bar placement, belt/shoes, knee collapse.

## 034 俄挺进阶
- Camera/view: side 3/4 slightly above hands.
- Equipment: floor or low parallettes.
- Frame 1: tuck planche lean, scapula protracted.
- Frame 2: advanced tuck/one-leg extension.
- Frame 3: straddle or near-full planche, locked arms.
- Primary red: 三角肌, 胸肌.
- Secondary orange: 核心.
- Avoid: rings, bent-elbow dip posture.

## 035 俯身靠墙划船
- Camera/view: side 3/4 showing wall/handle path.
- Equipment: wall anchor/fixed handles or band.
- Frame 1: body angled back, arms extended, heels grounded.
- Frame 2: elbows bend backward, scapulae retract.
- Frame 3: chest closer to wall/handles, elbows behind torso.
- Primary red: 背阔肌, 菱形肌.
- Secondary orange: 肱二头.
- Avoid: seated row machine, shrugging.
