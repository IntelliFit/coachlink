# batch-02 frame spec

Global: CoachLink v4 anatomy model; one horizontal 3-key-frame image per action; red primary, orange secondary; no text/labels/watermark.

## 006 俄挺
- Camera/view: 3/4 front, slightly low, full body.
- Equipment: floor only.
- Frame 1: tuck planche setup, hands beside hips, elbows locked, shoulders protracted, knees tucked.
- Frame 2: advanced tuck/straddle transition, hips near shoulder height, torso leaning forward.
- Frame 3: full planche hold, body horizontal, elbows locked, knees locked, feet pointed.
- Primary red: anterior deltoids, triceps, serratus anterior, rectus abdominis.
- Secondary orange: forearms, biceps stabilizers, upper chest, lats, glutes.
- Avoid: bent elbows, shoes, superhero proportions.

## 007 负重臂屈伸-弹力带辅助
- Camera/view: 3/4 front, full body.
- Equipment: dip bars, hanging plate, resistance band under knees/feet.
- Frame 1: top support, band stretched, torso slightly forward.
- Frame 2: eccentric midpoint/bottom, elbows about 90 degrees, band assisting.
- Frame 3: press to top support, elbows locked, chest lifted.
- Primary red: pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, triceps.
- Secondary orange: serratus anterior, lower trapezius, forearms.
- Avoid: rings, band hiding body anatomy.

## 008 前水平
- Camera/view: side with slight front angle.
- Equipment: straight pull-up bar.
- Frame 1: active hang, legs slightly tucked, pelvis beginning posterior tilt.
- Frame 2: raise transition, torso leaning back, hips rising, legs extending.
- Frame 3: full front lever, straight horizontal body under bar, arms locked.
- Primary red: lats, teres major, posterior deltoids, rectus abdominis.
- Secondary orange: biceps, forearms, lower trapezius, glutes, hip flexors.
- Avoid: bent knees final, chin-up curl posture, chest primary highlight.

## 009 手肘松解
- Camera/view: close 3/4 front from shoulders to hands.
- Equipment: massage/lacrosse ball on wall/table.
- Frame 1: forearm positioned on ball near elbow, relaxed shoulder.
- Frame 2: pressure roll midpoint, forearm glides, wrist rotates slightly.
- Frame 3: opposite-side forearm exposure, controlled pressure maintained.
- Primary red: forearm flexor/extensor mass around elbow, brachialis/brachioradialis.
- Secondary orange: distal biceps/triceps area, wrist flexors/extensors.
- Avoid: pain/gore/bruising, medical labels.

## 010 提膝双力臂
- Camera/view: 3/4 front, full body, bar centered.
- Equipment: straight pull-up bar.
- Frame 1: active hang, knees slightly bent, overhand grip.
- Frame 2: transition, knees drive upward, chest pulls to bar, elbows rotate over.
- Frame 3: support above bar, elbows nearly locked, torso upright, knees slightly raised.
- Primary red: lats, biceps, brachialis, triceps, rectus abdominis.
- Secondary orange: forearms, rear deltoids, lower trapezius, hip flexors, serratus.
- Avoid: exaggerated kip, rings, dip-only finish.
