# batch-09 frame spec

Global: v4 persona, 3-key-frame horizontal image, action-only red/orange highlights.

## 041 杠铃深蹲
- Camera/view: front 3/4, full body, slight low camera.
- Equipment: Olympic barbell on upper back.
- Frame 1: standing tall, bar on back, feet shoulder-width.
- Frame 2: eccentric descent, hips back/down, knees track toes.
- Frame 3: standing lockout.
- Primary red: 股四头肌.
- Secondary orange: 臀大肌, 核心.
- Avoid: rack, shoes, extra colors.

## 042 杠铃臀推
- Camera/view: side 3/4, bench visible.
- Equipment: flat bench, padded barbell across hips.
- Frame 1: upper back on bench, hips low, knees bent.
- Frame 2: hips rising, torso rotates toward horizontal.
- Frame 3: hips fully extended, torso/thighs aligned.
- Primary red: 臀大肌.
- Secondary orange: 腘绳肌.
- Avoid: quadriceps dominance, clutter.

## 043 杠铃弯举
- Camera/view: front 3/4, full body but upper body emphasized.
- Equipment: straight barbell, supinated grip.
- Frame 1: arms extended, bar near thighs.
- Frame 2: bar around lower chest, elbows flexing.
- Frame 3: bar near upper chest, upper arms stable.
- Primary red: 肱二头肌.
- Secondary orange: 肱肌.
- Avoid: torso swing, shoulder raise.

## 044 高翻
- Camera/view: side 3/4, full body.
- Equipment: Olympic barbell.
- Frame 1: start from floor, hips hinged, chest lifted.
- Frame 2: explosive extension, bar passing thighs, shoulders shrug.
- Frame 3: catch on front shoulders, elbows high, knees soft.
- Primary red: 臀大肌, 股四头肌.
- Secondary orange: 斜方肌.
- Avoid: snatch overhead, platform markings.

## 045 高杠深蹲
- Camera/view: front 3/4, upright torso visible.
- Equipment: barbell high on traps.
- Frame 1: standing high-bar setup.
- Frame 2: knees forward, hips down, torso vertical.
- Frame 3: standing lockout.
- Primary red: 股四头肌.
- Secondary orange: 臀大肌, 核心.
- Avoid: low-bar back angle, rack, shoes.
