# batch-01 frame spec

Global: horizontal 3-key-frame sequence, CoachLink v4 anatomy model, barefoot faceless translucent body/skeleton, action-only red/orange muscle highlights, no text/labels/watermark.

## 001 负重臂屈伸
- Camera/view: front 3/4, slight low angle, full body.
- Equipment: parallel dip bars, dip belt with hanging plate.
- Frame 1: top support, elbows nearly locked, torso slightly forward, legs crossed, plate hanging centered.
- Frame 2: controlled lowering, elbows about 90 degrees, shoulders depressed, chest open.
- Frame 3: press back toward top support, arms extending, body stable.
- Primary red: triceps, lower chest, anterior deltoids.
- Secondary orange: core, forearms, upper-back/scapular stabilizers.
- Avoid: kipping, shoes, face detail, unrelated highlights.

## 002 负重引体（底部暂停）
- Camera/view: rear 3/4 with side visibility, full body and bar.
- Equipment: pull-up bar, dip belt with hanging plate.
- Frame 1: bottom dead-hang pause, arms extended, shoulders active, weight still.
- Frame 2: pull midpoint, elbows drive down/back, scapulae depressed/retracted.
- Frame 3: chin above bar, lats compressed, body controlled.
- Primary red: latissimus dorsi, biceps/brachialis.
- Secondary orange: forearms, rear deltoids, mid/lower traps, rhomboids, core.
- Avoid: swing, kipping, chest-dominant highlight.

## 003 弹力带肩部热身
- Camera/view: front view, full body, band visible at shoulder height.
- Equipment: light resistance band.
- Frame 1: arms straight forward at shoulder height, hands shoulder-width.
- Frame 2: band pull-apart midpoint, arms opening outward, scapulae retracting.
- Frame 3: arms wide at shoulder height, band stretched, external rotation controlled.
- Primary red: deltoids, rotator cuff area.
- Secondary orange: rear deltoids, upper/mid back stabilizers, forearms.
- Avoid: heavy strain, torso twist, thick band hiding anatomy.

## 004 手腕热身
- Camera/view: front 3/4 upper-body view, hands large enough to show wrists.
- Equipment: none.
- Frame 1: elbows bent before torso, palms down, wrists straight.
- Frame 2: controlled wrist flexion/extension, forearms stable.
- Frame 3: wrist rotation/end range, hands turned symmetrically.
- Primary red: forearm flexors/extensors.
- Secondary orange: hand intrinsic region, light elbow stabilizers.
- Avoid: painful extremes, medical gore, labels.

## 005 臂屈伸
- Camera/view: front 3/4, full body.
- Equipment: parallel dip bars, no weight.
- Frame 1: top support, arms extended, shoulders packed down.
- Frame 2: lowering midpoint, elbows bend backward, torso forward.
- Frame 3: press back toward support, elbows extending.
- Primary red: chest, triceps.
- Secondary orange: anterior deltoids, forearms, serratus/scapular stabilizers, core.
- Avoid: weighted belt, shoulder collapse, shoes.
